# Audit of the Phase 1 profiling suite — 2026-09-12

Scope: the five experiment scripts, the priors exporter, the experiment logs and the report sources in this
repository, last touched 2026-07-28, read against the dataset as deployed on `ih-condor` (MICCAI release,
3,492 scans) and against what the model-training repository established since (`DATASET_FACTS.md`). Every
defect below was checked on real files, not inferred from the code alone.

## 1. What is wrong, with the mechanism

| # | Where | Defect | Effect on the published numbers |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `exp_004_hu_radiodensity.py` | The CT is reoriented to RAS with its real affine (the raw volumes are stored **LPS**), but the 4D mask is passed to `as_closest_canonical` with its **own identity affine**, so it is not reoriented. The two arrays keep the same shape and pass the shape check, mirrored in x and y relative to each other. | HU values were sampled at the mirror image of every lesion. All absolute HU statistics, the "recommended windows" and the contrast deltas are measurements of the wrong voxels. The corrected summary in the training repo dropped the absolute HU figures on 2026-09-05 as "physiologically implausible" without naming this cause; the contrast delta it kept is affected by the same defect. |
| 2 | `exp_005_morphology_noise_pruning.py` | Voxel spacing is read from the **mask header**, which carries an identity affine and zooms of (1, 1, 1) mm. The CT header, which holds the real spacing (e.g. 0.524 × 0.524 × 1.0 mm), is never opened. | Every "mm³" and "mm" figure is in voxel units mislabelled as millimetres; sphericity mixes a voxel-count surface with a voxel volume; the aspect ratio z/xy ignores anisotropy. Only the pure voxel-count percentiles (`recommended_min_size_voxels`) are unit-free. Components use 6-connectivity; the deployed pipeline uses 26. |
| 3 | `exp_003_spatial_density_priors.py` | Centroids are fractions of the **scan's voxel grid**, and masks are resampled to 128³ ignoring spacing. The z fraction therefore depends on how many slices the scan has and where the FOV starts, not on anatomy; the figure title hard-codes "N=7,687". | The train-vs-val centroid deltas and cosine similarities compare FOV geometry as much as pathology location. Usable only as a coarse sketch. The TotalSegmentator lobe masks now cached for all 3,492 scans on `ih-condor` make a lung-normalised version cheap. |
| 4 | `exp_001_dataset_disparity_leakage.py` | Instance counts come from `entity_counts` in `dataset.json`, which the **test** split does not carry; the code substitutes 1. | The test-split instance statistics in the logs and the summary are fabricated (already removed from the training repo's copy on 2026-09-05). Train/val counts, the 1.91× disparity, the co-occurrence matrix and the patient overlap are computed from real fields and were re-verified in `DATASET_FACTS.md` §2 and §4. Note that 414 train entries are named `valid_*`; the patient id is still the second token, so the overlap audit is unaffected. |
| 5 | `exp_002_nlp_prompt_syntax.py` | Token counts are a hand-written heuristic (word length and suffix rules), not the tokenizer of any model used; the "cases 1–50 vs 51–200" split assumes the validation list is ordered paper-release first, which was never checked. | The 77/128-token truncation rates and the "BPE expansion" are guesses. Word counts, comma rates and the spatial-locator vocabulary are fine. |
| 6 | all mask-based scripts | Masks are loaded with `get_fdata()` (float64) in up to 32 worker processes: a 4-finding 512×512×300 mask is 2.5 GB per worker. No SLURM launcher exists. Outputs go to `../data/phase_1/`, outside the repository and untracked. | The runs cannot be repeated on the login node, and none of the numbers in the logs or the report can be regenerated or diffed. |
| 7 | documentation | `.agents/` duplicates the training repo's governance; `PHASE_1_DATA_ANALYSIS_SUMMARY.md` here diverges by 286 lines from the corrected copy in the training repo; README links use `file://` paths; the Overleaf report and the presentation carry the numbers from defects 1–5. | Two sources of truth for Phase 1, one of them known-wrong. |

## 2. What survives

The five questions are the right ones: split composition and annotation disparity, text, spatial priors,
radiodensity, morphology and size. The `dataset.json`-only results (counts, 1.91×, co-occurrence, patient
overlap) are correct and are already carried by `DATASET_FACTS.md`. Nothing computed from the masks should
be quoted until it is regenerated.

## 3. Recommended redo (one pass, three tables, everything else derived)

1. **One loader.** Reuse the training repo's Universal Mask Policy (`scripts/common/orientation.py`): a mask
   takes its parent CT's affine before any reorientation; both are brought to RAS together; spacing comes
   from the CT header. Load masks as `uint8`, one finding channel at a time.
2. **One extraction job, sharded on SLURM (CPU),** over all 3,492 scans, writing three tables into the
   repository (Parquet or CSV, a few MB):
   * `scans`: split, shape, spacing, orientation code, intensity regime (−8192 padding present or not),
     lung bounding box from the cached lobe masks, number of findings.
   * `findings`: split, category, sentence, real token count (the Qwen3-Embedding tokenizer VoxTell uses),
     instance count recomputed with 26-connectivity, voxel count, volume in mm³, bounding box in mm, centroid
     in lung-box-normalised coordinates, lobe fractions, HU percentiles inside the mask and in a 3 mm shell
     around it, computed on correctly aligned arrays.
   * `components`: one row per connected component with voxels, mm³, extent, sphericity from a marching-cubes
     surface, and its lung-normalised centroid.
3. **Analyses as small scripts over the tables**, each producing one figure and one table for the report:
   disparity (instances and volumes, train vs val vs test where the field exists), lesion size distribution
   per category (the scale polarity), spatial priors per category in lung coordinates with train/val
   agreement, HU profiles, text length and vocabulary shift, image-level statistics, co-occurrence, patient
   overlap. Qualitative pictures come from the 3D renderer in the training repo (`scripts/render`).
4. **Report numbers regenerated from the tables** and the Phase 1 chapter of the technical report updated
   where a number changes; the old Overleaf report and presentation archived, not maintained.
5. **Repository hygiene:** drop the duplicated `.agents/` governance in favour of a pointer to the training
   repo; keep a bounded set of files (`profiling/extract.py`, `profiling/tables.py`, `profiling/figures/`,
   one launcher, one README).
